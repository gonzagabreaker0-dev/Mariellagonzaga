# Color Grade AI

Give footage cinematic looks with color adjustments.

Category: Color, Audio & AI Video Generation (skill #22)

## Workflow

1. Analyze exposure, white balance, and contrast
2. Apply a base correction first, then a creative look
3. Use ffmpeg filters (eq, colorbalance, curves, lut3d) or LUTs
4. Provide before/after frames
5. Export graded video

## Notes

- Confirm inputs (source files, brand assets, target platform) before starting.
- Prefer ffmpeg / Remotion / Playwright where available; check they are installed first.
- Keep intermediate and output files in a dedicated project folder.
