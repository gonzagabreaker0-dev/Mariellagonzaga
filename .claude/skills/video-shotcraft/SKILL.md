# Video Shotcraft

Turn websites and apps into cinematic product videos.

Category: Animations & Motion Graphics (skill #8)

## Workflow

1. Identify the key flows and features to showcase
2. Capture screens or recordings (Playwright works well for sites)
3. Plan shots: zooms, pans, device mockups, callouts
4. Compose and sequence shots with music/voiceover
5. Render and review for pacing

## Notes

- Confirm inputs (source files, brand assets, target platform) before starting.
- Prefer ffmpeg / Remotion / Playwright where available; check they are installed first.
- Keep intermediate and output files in a dedicated project folder.
